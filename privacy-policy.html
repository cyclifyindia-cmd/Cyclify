Privacy Policy

Effective 4 August 2026. This policy describes how CYCLIFY INDIA handles personal information on cyclify.in.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery address, account identifier, order details, support messages, reviews and technical information such as browser, device and website usage data.

How we use information

  • To create and secure customer accounts.
  • To process payments, orders, deliveries, exchanges and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with tax, accounting and legal requirements.
  • To improve the website, product catalogue and customer experience.
  • To send essential service communications and, where permitted, marketing communications.

Payments and service providers

Payments are processed by authorised payment service providers. CYCLIFY INDIA does not intentionally store complete card, UPI PIN or banking credentials. We may use service providers for authentication, hosting, analytics, communication, order management and delivery, sharing only information reasonably required for those services.

Cookies and authentication

The website may use cookies and similar storage for account sessions, cart functionality, security, preferences and analytics. Google sign-in and Firebase services may process account and technical data according to their own privacy terms.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably required for the purposes above and applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. You may also withdraw optional marketing consent.
