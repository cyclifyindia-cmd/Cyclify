Bicycle Maintenance Hacks That Actually Work

The most useful maintenance hacks are simple inspections performed regularly. They prevent expensive wear without replacing correct workshop procedures or manufacturer torque specifications.

The quick pre-ride check

Squeeze both brakes, check tyre pressure, spin each wheel and confirm that quick releases or thru-axles are secure. Look for cuts, embedded glass, loose spokes and leaking tubeless sealant.

Lift the bicycle slightly and listen for loose parts. A new noise is easier to diagnose before it becomes a failure.

Clean and lubricate correctly

Wipe the chain before applying bicycle chain lubricant, use only a small drop per roller and remove the excess. Lubricant on brake rotors or rims can seriously reduce braking.

After wet rides, dry the bicycle and clean road grit from the drivetrain. Avoid directing high-pressure water at bearings, suspension seals and electronic connections.

Know when to use a workshop

Use a torque wrench for carbon components and manufacturer-specified fasteners. Seek professional service for brake bleeding, damaged carbon, bearing play, wheel cracks, persistent gear skipping or steering looseness.

Record chain replacement, sealant refresh and major service dates. Timely chain replacement can extend cassette and chainring life substantially.
