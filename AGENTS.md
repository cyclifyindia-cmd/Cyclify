# Cyclify product publishing workflow

For every new product, include SEO and Merchant feed work without waiting for a separate request.

- Confirm the selling price, MRP, stock and selectable variants with Cyclify. Do not substitute another seller's availability for Cyclify's own stock.
- Use verified model-specific specifications and genuine product photos. Never invent compatibility, reviews, ratings, GTINs or delivery promises.
- Preserve Cyclify's header, purchase controls and category display conventions. Do not publish supplier contact details or imply that a reference retailer is the seller.
- Keep the canonical product record, allowlist, category links, image assets and trusted payment catalogue consistent. Validate the selected options and full price server-side.
- Generate and test the canonical page, structured product data, social image, sitemap, search index and Merchant feed. Verify the live page and feed after publishing.
- Refresh Merchant Center and request Search Console indexing when access permits. Report pending Google processing honestly; never guarantee rankings or indexing.
- Do not alter existing shipping or return policies without confirmation.
